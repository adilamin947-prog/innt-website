About INNT

A registered operator first. A chauffeur service by standard.

INNT was built on a simple premise: private hire should mean exactly what it says — licensed, accountable, and run to a standard you can check, not just trust.

Our Approach

Every credential is public for a reason.

Most private hire operators treat their licence and registration as paperwork. We treat it as the standard we're held to. INNT LTD is registered under SIC 49320 — Taxi Operation — and every driver, vehicle and journey we run sits under that registration.

That means a driver you can check, a vehicle that's been inspected on schedule, and a fare that was agreed before the journey started — not adjusted afterward.
