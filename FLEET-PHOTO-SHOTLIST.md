# INNT Fleet — Photo Shoot List

Client ko ye brief bhej sakte hain seedha. Teeno categories (Saloon, Executive, MPV) ka style same rakhna hai taake website pe consistent lagein.

---

## General Rules (In sab pe apply hoga)

- **Gaari clean honi chahiye** — wash + interior vacuum shoot se pehle, koi dust/fingerprint na ho
- **Time of day:** Golden hour (subah 7-8am ya shaam 5-6pm) — soft light, koi harsh shadow nahi. Dopeher ki tez dhoop avoid karein
- **Background:** Plain/professional location — office building, hotel entrance, ya clean road/parking. Koi clutter, dusra traffic, ya branding wale boards background mein na ho
- **Orientation:** Har gaari ki **landscape (horizontal)** photos leni hain — website cards horizontal hain
- **Consistency:** Teeno gaariyon ki photos same angle/style/background se lein taake set jaisa lage, random na lage
- **Resolution:** Phone se lein to kam az kam 12MP, achi roshni mein — website pe sharp dikhna chahiye

---

## Per Vehicle — Kya Shots Chahiye (har gaari ke liye ye 4 shots minimum)

### 1. Hero Shot (Exterior — 3/4 Front Angle)
- Gaari ka front-side angle (jaise showroom photos hoti hain) — driver-side front corner se
- Poori gaari frame mein aani chahiye, thoda neeche se angle achha lagta hai
- **Ye wahi photo hai jo fleet card ke top pe lagegi — sabse important shot**

### 2. Side Profile Shot
- Seedha side se, gaari ki poori length dikhni chahiye
- Interior/about page pe use ho sakti hai

### 3. Interior Shot
- Back seat se front ki taraf, ya front seat se back — jo bhi zyada spacious/premium dikhe
- Leather/seat quality, legroom clearly dikhna chahiye
- Executive aur MPV ke liye ye especially important hai (comfort sell karna hai)

### 4. Detail Shot (Optional but Recommended)
- Koi ek premium detail — jaise dashboard, ya driver door se number plate ka close-up (professionalism dikhane ke liye)

---

## Category-Specific Notes

**Saloon**
- Focus: compact, sharp, efficient — solo/2-passenger transfers ke liye
- Ek clean corporate/airport-type location pe shoot karein

**Executive**
- Focus: premium feel — thoda zyada "polished" angle, better lighting agar mumkin ho
- Agar driver bhi shoot mein include karna ho (suit mein khada, door khol raha), ye bohot achha impression dega — optional hai

**MPV**
- Focus: space — side door khula hua ek shot zaroor lein taake seating capacity dikhe
- Agar mumkin ho, group ke saath ek lifestyle shot (family/colleagues boarding) — optional

---

## Total Shot Count
- 3 vehicles × 4 shots = **12 photos minimum**
- Sab ek hi session mein, same location/lighting mein shoot ho jayen to best hoga
